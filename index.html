The front end is the product

Buying good gear is not hard, and neither is plugging it in. What separates one vendor from the next on a show this well-established is whether a detail from last year quietly falls off this year's pick list. We run the front end on software we built ourselves: email threads linked into the job so the whole team sees the same conversation, advances and show packets generated automatically and handed to crew as live links we update in real time, crew staffed and briefed through an app, and a tech call before every show. No one arrives asking what we're doing today.

Engineered twice, then cloned forward

The price you approve is the price that's right

We deliberately separate the sales order from the bill of materials. After a scope is priced, it goes through an engineering pass — our team reads the drawings, the threads and the call recaps, and reconciles what was sold against what actually has to ship. Anything missed gets added on our side of the line, not yours, and the corrected build is saved against your account. Next April we clone it forward instead of rebuilding it from memory. That is how a recurring show stops drifting.

Section 2.3 — Handoff: format, connector, resolution, frame rate“What you require at the handoff — signal format, connector type, resolution and frame rate.”
Format and connector: 12G-SDI terminated BNC, or single-mode fiber to our rack with the break-out on our side.
Preferred resolution and frame rate: 2688 × 1512, 60 fps progressive — a native 16:9 canvas that maps one-to-one across the pair of walls with no scaling loss.
Also accepted: 1920 × 1080p at 60 fps over 3G-SDI, which we upscale and map in our processing.
Either signal is accepted as a finished program feed. We require no genlock or reference from your control room.

On signal loss: the wall holds last frame rather than dropping to noise or snow, and if the feed does not return it goes to a black slate. It will never show an error dialog, a desktop, or colour bars to your bowl. The LED Wall Technician is on headset for the entire show and is the person who makes that call.
